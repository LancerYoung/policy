我们想做的，是一款"会写就会打"的输入法——安静、克制，且绝不窥探你。

嘎嘎笔画-智能隐私输入法（键盘名「嘎嘎」）诞生于一个很朴素的观察：身边有太多人不擅长拼音——记不住、方言重、或是提笔忘字。他们需要的不是又一套复杂的输入方案，而是一个门槛足够低、又足够让人放心的工具。

于是我们选择了一条"轻"的路：只做五种基本笔画（横竖撇捺折），按书写顺序输入；把所有解码、联想与学习都放在你自己的设备上完成；不请求任何权限，也不联网。你敲下的每个字，只属于你自己。

🦆 独立开发 🍎 iOS 16+ 🔌 纯离线 · 零权限 🚫 无广告 · 无追踪

We set out to build an input method that's as simple as "if you can write it, you can type it" — quiet, restrained, and never prying into your data.

Gaga Stroke (keyboard name "Gaga") began with a simple observation: too many people around us are not comfortable with Pinyin — they can't memorise it, speak a strong dialect, or simply forget how a character is pronounced. What they need isn't another complicated input scheme, but a tool with a low barrier and a strong sense of trust.

So we took the "light" path: only the five basic strokes (horizontal, vertical, left-falling, dot, turning), typed in writing order; all decoding, prediction, and learning done on your own device; no permissions requested, and no network access. Every character you type belongs to you alone.

🦆 Independently built 🍎 iOS 16+ 🔌 Offline · zero permissions 🚫 No ads · no tracking

我们的三条原则

🔒隐私优先

键盘扩展零权限、零网络、零数据收集。字频学习、词库匹配、候选排序全部在设备本地完成。我们不用任何分析或广告 SDK，也不会把你的输入上传到任何服务器。

🔌纯离线

不依赖"允许完全访问"，所有功能在关闭该权限时就已完整可用。可选的主 App 联网仅用于 App Store 购买 / 恢复，以及由你自己的 iCloud 承载的词同步。

🪶克制极简

五种笔画键加大字距防误触，候选栏横向滑动选字，没有资讯流、没有弹窗、没有多余按钮。打开就是键盘，关闭就消失——把注意力还给你要打的字。

Our Three Principles

🔒Privacy first

The keyboard extension has zero permissions, zero network access, and collects zero data. Word-frequency learning, dictionary matching, and candidate ranking all run locally. We use no analytics or ad SDKs, and never upload what you type to any server.

🔌Truly offline

No "Allow Full Access" required — every feature is fully usable with it turned off. The optional main-app network use is limited to App Store purchase/restore and word sync carried by your own iCloud.

🪶Restrained & minimal

Five oversized stroke keys with wide spacing to avoid mistaps and a horizontally swipeable candidate bar. No feed, no pop-ups, no clutter. Open it and it's a keyboard; close it and it's gone — returning your attention to the words you're typing.

做嘎嘎的人

嘎嘎由一位独立开发者设计与维护，从笔画引擎、词格解码与语言模型，到键盘交互与主 App 的每一处细节，都是自己一点点打磨出来的。没有大团队，也没有外部依赖内核——这让每一个决定都能以"对用户是否更好、是否更安全"为唯一标准。

Who Makes Gaga

Gaga is designed and maintained by an independent developer. From the stroke engine, lattice decoding, and language model, to the keyboard interaction and every detail of the main app, it has been polished step by step. There is no large team and no third-party engine behind it — which lets every decision be judged by a single standard: is it better and safer for the user?
