FAQ

常见问题Questions, answered

不会拼音、不认字，能用吗？I don't know Pinyin. Can I use it?

可以。嘎嘎按汉字的书写顺序输入五种基本笔画（横竖撇捺折），完全不依赖读音。会写这个字，就能把它打出来。Yes. Gaga takes the five basic strokes (horizontal, vertical, left-falling, dot, turning) in writing order and doesn't rely on pronunciation at all. If you can write the character, you can type it.

需要开启「允许完全访问」吗？Do I need to enable "Allow Full Access"?

不需要，也不需要任何权限。嘎嘎所有功能（笔画输入、词格整句、皮肤、大字模式、触感、iCloud 词同步）在关闭完全访问的状态下都正常工作。键盘扩展默认处于系统离线隔离，无法联网。No — and no permissions at all are needed. Every feature (stroke input, lattice input, themes, large-text mode, haptics, iCloud sync) works with Full Access disabled. The keyboard is offline by default under iOS sandbox isolation.

我的输入数据去哪了？Where does my input data go?

哪里都不去。键盘不收集、不追踪、不上传任何输入内容。字频学习仅保存在本机 App Group 容器内，用于个性化候选排序，不会离开你的设备，也不与任何第三方共享。Nowhere. The keyboard collects, tracks, and uploads nothing. Word-frequency learning stays only in the on-device App Group container for personalized ranking — it never leaves your device and is shared with no third party.

大字模式是什么？What is large-text mode?

专为长辈与视力不佳的用户设计。开启后第一个候选字会以大字号显示在键盘左上角，看得清、点得准；问号等不常用键会隐藏，把空间让给候选。免费可用。Designed for elders and low-vision users. When enabled, the top candidate appears large in the top-left of the keyboard — easier to read and tap; less-used keys such as "?" are hidden to give candidates more room. Free to use.

如何恢复购买 / 申请退款？How do I restore or refund a purchase?

更换设备或重装后，打开主 App 点击「恢复已购项目」即可同步。所有支付由 Apple 处理，退款请通过 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请，开发者无法直接退款。After switching devices or reinstalling, open the main app and tap "Restore Purchases". All payments are handled by Apple; request refunds via reportaproblem.apple.com — the developer cannot refund directly.

免费版能用什么？What can the free version do?

笔画输入、分词键、全部手势、大字模式、经典皮肤、键盘外观自定义、iCloud 用户词同步、词格整句解码均免费可用；退格左滑清空、剪贴板历史与进阶 / 高级皮肤需会员解锁。Stroke input, the segment key, all gestures, large-text mode, classic themes, appearance control, iCloud word sync, and lattice decoding are all free. Backspace-swipe clear, clipboard history, and advanced/premium themes require membership.
