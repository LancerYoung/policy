隐私保护政策

最近更新：2026 年 10 月 10 日

本政策说明「HotSeat」（热座）App（以下简称"本应用"）如何收集、使用与保护你的信息。本应用是一款单机派对聊天游戏，我们坚持"最小收集"原则。

一、我们收集什么

本应用不要求注册账号，也不收集任何能直接识别你个人的信息（如姓名、手机号、邮箱、地理位置、通讯录、相册、相机、IDFA/广告标识符）。

仅收集以下匿名数据，用于维持产品运行与改进体验：

数据类型说明是否关联身份
匿名安装 ID随机生成、仅存本机，用于统计"有多少人在玩、独立玩家数（DAU/MAU）"否
玩法聚合数据开局/散场次数、领域命中、成就解锁等统计，不含你输入的具体对话内容否
崩溃日志App 意外崩溃时的崩溃种类、信号、调用栈地址（仅版本/系统/栈地址，无个人信息）否

你与 AI 角色的对话、你选择的话题选项均在设备本地处理，不会上传到我们的服务器，也不会用于训练。

二、我们怎么用这些数据

  • 匿名聚合统计：了解活跃度、玩法分布，用于决定"接下来写哪些剧本"；
  • 崩溃诊断：发现线上崩溃、定位问题版本，提升稳定性；
  • 绝不用于跨应用追踪、广告投放或任何画像营销。

三、数据存储与第三方

匿名数据经由 Supabase 托管（仅后端基础设施，数据仅含上文所述匿名字段）。所有上报均为后台静默进行，失败即丢弃，不影响你的游戏体验。

四、可选的第三方 AI 服务

本应用默认使用设备端模型生成台词。如果你在设置中自行配置了第三方 AI 服务商（如 OpenAI、DeepSeek 等）及自己的密钥，你与角色的对话内容将按你的配置发送给该服务商处理，适用该服务商自己的隐私政策；不配置则全程设备本地生成，对话不出设备。

五、内购与支付

本应用的订阅、剧本包、买断等均通过 Apple App Store 内购完成。支付由 Apple 处理，我们不收集、不存储任何支付信息（信用卡号、账单等）。订阅状态仅以"是否订阅/到期时间"等形式保存在你本机。

六、你的权利：删除数据

你可以在 App 内「设置 → 数据 → 删除我的数据」一键删除本机安装 ID 对应的所有匿名统计与崩溃记录（云端同步删除）。

如需进一步协助，可邮件联系：lancer.yang@foxmail.com，或通过支持页面联系我们。

七、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

八、政策变更

如本政策有重大变更，会在应用内或本页面更新并说明生效日期。

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how the HotSeat app (the "App") collects, uses and protects your information. HotSeat is an offline party conversation game, and we follow a strict data-minimisation approach.

1. What we collect

The App requires no account and collects no directly identifying information (name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, camera, IDFA / advertising identifiers).

We collect only the following anonymous data, to keep the product running and improve it:

DataPurposeLinked to identity?
Anonymous install IDRandomly generated, stored only on your device; counts how many people play (DAU/MAU)No
Aggregated gameplay statsSession counts, topic-category hits, achievements — never the actual content of your conversationsNo
Crash logsCrash type, signal and stack addresses when the App crashes (version/system/stack only, no personal data)No

Your conversations with the AI characters and the options you pick are processed on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers and never used for training.

2. How we use this data

  • Anonymous aggregate statistics: understand activity and play patterns to decide which scenarios to write next;
  • Crash diagnostics: catch crashes in the wild and fix the affected versions;
  • Never for cross-app tracking, advertising, or profiling of any kind.

3. Storage & third parties

Anonymous data is hosted via Supabase (backend infrastructure only; the data contains only the anonymous fields described above). All uploads happen silently in the background and are simply discarded on failure — your game is never affected.

4. Optional third-party AI providers

By default, dialogue is generated by the on-device model. If you configure your own third-party AI provider (e.g. OpenAI, DeepSeek) and API key in Settings, your conversations with the characters are sent to that provider as you configured, under that provider's own privacy policy. Without such a configuration, everything stays on your device.

5. Purchases & payments

Subscriptions, scenario packs and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment is processed by Apple; we never collect or store any payment information (card numbers, billing details, etc.). Subscription status is stored on your device only (subscribed or not, expiry time).

6. Your rights: deleting data

In the App, go to Settings → Data → Delete My Data to remove all anonymous statistics and crash records tied to this device's install ID (deleted from the cloud as well).

For anything else, email lancer.yang@foxmail.com or reach us via the support page.

7. Children

HotSeat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page, with the effective date noted.
