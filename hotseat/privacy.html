隐私保护政策

最近更新：2026 年 9 月 28 日

HotSeat 是一款单机的对话游戏。我们把话说在前面：我们不收集你的任何数据——不需要注册账号，不需要登录，不需要联网远程存的任何东西。

一、我们不收集什么

答案很简单：什么都不收集。

  • 没有账号系统，不需要注册、登录或邮箱；
  • 不读取你的通讯录、照片、麦克风、相机、定位、日历或健康数据；
  • App 不申请上述任何系统权限；
  • 不含任何统计、广告、归因或行为追踪 SDK；
  • 不使用 IDFA，也不会跨域追踪你。

二、游戏数据存在哪里

你的每一局游戏、人物图鉴进度、历史对话记录、设置项（比如是否开启背景音乐、选择了哪种界面语言），全部只保存在你这台设备的本地存储里。我们没有服务器用来存你的游戏数据，也没有办法读到它。

删除 App 即清除全部本地数据。

三、AI 台词是怎么生成的

角色台词由 Apple 提供的设备端模型（Apple Intelligence）在你的手机上即时生成与润色，不经过我们的服务器。你的作答和对话内容不会被上传到任何地方用于训练或分析。

当设备端模型不可用时，App 会直接播放预先写好的剧本文本，游戏体验不受影响。

四、唯一的联网行为

App 启动时可能向公共 CDN 请求一份「剧本目录」配置文件，以及在你需要某份新增剧本时下载对应的加密内容文件，用于更新本地剧本库。

这些请求是匿名的：请求中不包含账号、设备标识、IDFA 或任何身份信息，服务端也不会据此识别你。下载失败时 App 会静默使用本地已内置的剧本，不影响启动和游戏。

除此之外，App 不产生任何其它网络请求。

五、购买

App 内的一次性买断由 Apple 的 StoreKit 完成。整个支付流程由 Apple 处理，我们看不到也无法保存你的付款信息或身份信息。你可以在 App Store 的购买记录里管理或恢复已购项目。

六、儿童

HotSeat 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童的产品，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、政策变更

如果将来我们的数据处理方式有变化，会先更新本页内容，并确保你随时可以在这里看到当前生效的版本。

八、联系我们

关于隐私的任何问题，请通过支持页面上的方式联系我们。

HotSeat is an offline conversation game. Let’s put the short version first: we collect nothing about you. No account, no sign-in, no profile anywhere else.

1. What we collect

The honest answer: nothing.

  • No accounts, no registration, no email required;
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, calendar or health data;
  • The app requests none of those system permissions;
  • No analytics, advertising, attribution or behavioural tracking SDKs;
  • No IDFA and no cross-app tracking.

2. Where your game data lives

Every round you play, your Character Codex progress, full conversation history and settings (background music, interface language) are stored only in local storage on your own device. We run no server that holds your game data, and we have no way to read it.

Deleting the app removes all local data.

3. How dialogue is generated

Character lines are generated and rephrased on the spot by Apple’s on-device model (Apple Intelligence), running on your phone. They never pass through our servers. Your answers and the conversation are not uploaded anywhere for training or analysis.

When the on-device model is unavailable, the app plays the pre-written script text instead. Nothing breaks.

4. The one network request we make

On launch, the app may request a small content catalogue from a public CDN, and download an encrypted file when you open a newly released scenario — that is how we ship new material to your local library.

These requests are anonymous: they contain no account identifier, device identifier, IDFA or any personal information, and nothing in them can be used to single you out. If a download fails, the app quietly falls back to the scenarios already built in.

Outside of that, the app makes no other network requests.

5. Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Apple’s StoreKit. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store your payment or identity details. Manage or restore your purchase from your App Store account.

6. Children

HotSeat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If our practices ever change, this page changes first. The version published here is always the one in effect.

8. Contact

Any question about privacy: reach us through the support page.
