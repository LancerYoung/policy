常见问题

App 需要联网才能玩吗？

不需要。剧本文本全部内置在 App 里，飞行模式下也能完整玩一局。联网只用于获取后续新增的剧本文档。

为什么有些角色的台词感觉像在「现编」？

因为它确实是现场生成的。每句台词在说出来之前，会由 Apple 的设备端模型按照说话人的性格、当下情绪以及他对你的态度重新润色一遍。模型不可用时，会直接播放作者写好的原文。

界面语言怎么切换？

进入「设置 → 语言」，可以选择跟随系统，也可以手动指定一种界面语言。切换后立即生效，不需要重启。

「他们会记仇」是什么意思？

你在一局里留下的糗事会写进角色的档案，下一局开局时可能有人当场替你复述一遍。这个能力属于完整版的一部分。

历史记录里的对话能导出吗？

暂时还不能导出。你可以在 App 内随时翻阅每一局的完整对话记录和数据。

怎么恢复我的买断？

在 App 的「设置 → 解锁完整版」页面底部点「恢复购买」，系统会通过你的 App Store 账户验证。也可以直接在 App Store 账户的「购买记录」里确认。

会收集我的聊天内容吗？

不会。所有生成和判定都在你的设备上完成，我们没有服务器接收这些内容。详见隐私政策。

Frequently asked

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Every scenario ships inside the app, so a full round plays fine in Airplane mode. A connection is only used to fetch scenarios released after your version.

Why do some lines sound improvised?

Because they are. Right before a character speaks, the line is rephrased by Apple’s on-device model to match who is talking, their mood right now, and how much they currently like you. If the model isn’t available, the author’s original line is used instead.

How do I change the interface language?

Go to Settings → Language and either follow the system or pick one by hand. It applies immediately, no restart needed.

What does “they hold grudges” mean?

Your worst moments get filed away. Start a later round and someone may quote you back word for word. It’s part of the full version.

Can I export my transcripts?

Not yet. You can read the complete transcript and stats of any past round inside the app.

How do I restore my unlock?

Tap “Restore Purchases” at the bottom of Settings → Unlock Full Version; verification runs against your App Store account. You can also confirm it in your App Store purchase history.

Do you read my conversations?

No. Generation and scoring all happen on your device, and nothing is sent to a server of ours. See the privacy policy.
