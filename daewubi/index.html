FAQ

常见问题Questions, answered

需要开启"允许完全访问"吗？Do I need to enable "Allow Full Access"?

不需要，且强烈建议保持关闭。大鹅五笔所有功能（五笔输入、智能整句、皮肤、滑动、弹窗、触感）在关闭完全访问的状态下都正常工作。键盘扩展默认处于系统离线隔离，无法联网。No — and we strongly recommend keeping it off. All features (Wubi input, sentence input, themes, slide, popups, haptics) work normally with Full Access disabled. The keyboard is offline by default under iOS sandbox isolation.

我的输入数据去哪了？Where does my input data go?

哪里都不去。键盘不收集、不追踪、不上传任何输入内容。用户词频学习仅保存在本机 App Group 容器内，用于个性化候选排序，不会离开你的设备，也不与任何第三方共享。Nowhere. The keyboard collects, tracks, and uploads no input. User word-frequency learning stays only in the on-device App Group container for personalized ranking — it never leaves your device and is shared with no third party.

"智能整句"是什么？What is "sentence-level input"?

内置离线语言模型，输入一长串编码时自动整句分词、成词并排序，重码更少、输入更流畅。整句与候选均在设备本地完成，不上传。A built-in on-device language model that automatically segments, forms, and ranks whole sentences as you type — fewer duplicates, smoother input. Both the sentence and candidates are produced locally, with no upload.

如何恢复购买 / 退款？How do I restore or refund a purchase?

更换设备或重装后，打开主 App 点击「恢复购买」即可同步。所有支付由 Apple 处理，退款请通过 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请，开发者无法直接退款。After switching devices or reinstalling, open the main app and tap "Restore Purchases" to sync. All payments are handled by Apple; request refunds via reportaproblem.apple.com — the developer cannot refund directly.

免费版能用什么？What can the free version do?

完整五笔输入与词库、智能整句、3 套免费皮肤、滑动输入与上滑弹窗均可使用；Pro 皮肤与部分高级定制（弹窗定制、退格左滑、拼写纠错）需买断或订阅解锁。Full Wubi input and dictionaries, sentence-level input, 3 free themes, slide input and swipe-up popups are all usable. Pro themes and some advanced customizations (popup customization, backspace-swipe, spell-check) require Lifetime or subscription.
